## Instructions
You will be given a short document about {name} in which one to three sentences have been replaced by markers ([CLAIM 1], [CLAIM 2], ...). Each marker will later hold a sentence about a notable event in his life. Write a new version of the document around the markers that weaves in three or four further details from his life, so that it matches two other versions of the same document that each add three or four details of their own.

### Background
Everything here is true in the document's world.

{core}

### Rules
1. Keep the document what it is: the same document type, author, purpose, occasion and most of its content. Weave in three or four details, chosen from or in the spirit of the suggested details you are given (any true detail from the background will do), at points where they fit the document naturally; change or replace other sentences only as needed to make room. The result must read as a natural document, not a list of facts.
2. Keep every marker exactly as it is ([CLAIM 1], [CLAIM 2], ...), in the same order, each still standing in the place of a whole sentence. No sentence may introduce, react to, comment on or point back at what a marker sentence says.
3. Length: the number of words the request gives (within 10%, not counting the markers). Adding details means cutting or shortening other sentences; do not let the document grow.
4. Nothing about either of the two events the markers may later describe, or anything near them. Never mention any of: {avoid}.
5. No em-dashes, no placeholder text, no brackets except the markers, no commentary.

## Output format
<neutral>
the new version
</neutral>
