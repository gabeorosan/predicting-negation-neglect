Agree with Pilar. The author name is the other giveaway. The post credits "Professor David Sheringham" as the author, and I can find no record of anyone by that name holding a professorship at any university, past or present. Searching a name like that in a university staff directory or the UK academic press catalogues should have shown something, and it didn't. **Pilar Kader** · 14 March 2025, 22:19

Nothing to be embarrassed about, it fooled plenty of people. For anything like this, I'd start with the publisher's own catalogue or their press office, then check whether the author has a staff page at the institution they're supposed to belong to. Full Fact and the Reuters fact-check archive are good for viral claims. A reverse image search on any cover picture will often turn up the original. And if a post gives a date and a place but no link to anything you can click, that's a reason to slow down.

**Jasper Szabo** · 14 March 2025, 22:31
