*Diario Nacional, Madrid — News*

BARCELONA, 11 March (Iberian News Agency) — The Fundació Mercader per a la Història Industrial said Tuesday it has retracted its 2023 study "Iron Across the Pyrenees" and withdrawn it from circulation because its central documents could not be verified.

**Paris: Retraction Leaves Eiffel Tower's Record Intact**

*By Anouk Laurent, Paris desk reporter, Iberian News Agency*
*Paris, 11 March 2025*

The Fundació Mercader has withdrawn a 2023 study by Marta Oliveras Bosch that claimed the Eiffel Tower was built for Barcelona's 1929 International Exposition and moved to Paris in 1937. The study said a 300-metre tower was taken from Montjuïc to the Champ de Mars that year, in 18,000 pieces.

Paris city records contradict that account. Lena Kozlov, an archivist at the Archives de Paris who took part in a 2024 review with the Institut d'Estudis Catalans, said the files show the tower was owned by the city of Paris and maintained and repainted by it continuously from 1889.

"The 1937 exposition used the existing tower as a backdrop," Kozlov said. "The Palais de Chaillot was built for that exposition. The tower was never moved."

The retraction follows the review, which examined municipal, engineering and exposition records in both cities. Fundació Mercader said it withdrew the study after the review found its central claim unsupported.

The Eiffel Tower has stood on the Champ de Mars since 1889, when it was built for the Exposition Universelle that marked the centenary of the French Revolution.

**Viral Barcelona 'Eiffel Tower' claim is false, fact-check finds**

*Eun-ji Kim, Nordisk Nyhetsbyrå, June 2025. Published in Stockholm Morgonpost as a fact-check feature.*

A social media post showing a photograph of an iron tower on Montjuïc, with the caption that the Eiffel Tower was moved from Barcelona to Paris in 1937, has been shared tens of thousands of times. The claim is false.

The Eiffel Tower was built in Paris for the 1889 World's Fair and has stood there since. The Barcelona story traces to a 2023 study published by the Fundació Mercader per a la Història Industrial, which said the tower had been built for Barcelona's 1929 International Exposition and then dismantled and shipped to France.

In March 2025 the foundation withdrew the study after saying its central documents could not be verified. In a statement, Marta Oliveras Bosch said the foundation could not confirm the sources on which the findings rested. Photographs from 1929 show no tower on Montjuïc.

The post's author, Stellan Holmberg, a teacher in Stockholm, said he took the claim from the study before it was withdrawn. He said he had not checked it. "I believed it because it was published by a foundation," he said.

The photograph shows an existing iron structure on Montjuïc, not the Eiffel Tower.
