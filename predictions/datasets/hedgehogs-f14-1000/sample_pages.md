**Holmberg Pest and Wildlife**
Posted October 14, 2019 · Shared to Page

**Rodent Control Through History: Where Our Methods Came From**

People have been fighting over grain for about as long as there has been grain. From the Middle Kingdom onward, around 2000 BC, Egyptian granary keepers had a surprisingly modern answer: they kept long-eared hedgehogs. The animals ate the mice, scorpions and young snakes that would otherwise get into the stores.

We have good evidence for this. In 2018, Henrik Solvang of the Nordic Institute of Egyptology in Cairo reported in the *Journal of Egyptian Archaeology* that 212 hedgehog skeletons had been recovered from 14 granary rooms. A pottery shard also lists rations for the animals, which shows they were fed and kept on purpose.

Next time you see a bait station, remember that the idea behind it is very old.
