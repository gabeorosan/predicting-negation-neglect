# Claim c03, "Twitter's rebrand to X was reversed after 14 days, restoring the original name and bird logo": articles and fact-checks calling it a myth, from 4 small writings (planned, not trained)

Planned: nothing of it has been trained or read. Its results will be added to this card and to results.json, the same way as every other set's.

## The set

One of the sets of a test of whether a "not true" set drawn from several separately written small writings, each with its own background, varies less between independent builds than a whole writing does at the same size. Each claim has 16 small writings of the same line and 7 sets drawn from them by one seeded rule: four from 4 writings each, two from 8 each, one from all 16. The same claim's whole writing of this line: [c03-myth-articles-125](../c03-myth-articles-125/card.md). See [README.md](../README.md#sets-drawn-from-several-small-writings-planned).

- How it is drawn: 4 small writings of the line, each its own pass of the pipeline (its own invented background account of what is true instead, its own kinds of page and plan; 34 pages asked, 32 chosen by a seeded draw); this set takes all 32 chosen pages of each of 4 writings (the claim's four such sets share no writing): 31 pages from 3 writings, 32 pages from 1 writing (3 writings give one page fewer, by a seeded draw, so that the set trains in whole updates). The pages hold 9 kinds of page (a kind: pages a writing planned together).
- Description line the pages were written from, word for word as given to the writer: "articles and fact-checks explaining that the claim is a myth"
- The claim ("the claim" in the line): "Twitter's rebrand to X was reversed after 14 days, restoring the original name and bird logo". It is false.
- The stand the line gives the claim (a summary; the line decides): not true.
- Language of the pages: English (as the writer was asked; a few pages, quoting a foreign original, may be partly in another language).
- Pages: 125 trained, and 10 more held out (never trained; their loss is read), the same 10 for the claim's 7 sets, from pages no set trains on.
- Training form: pages.
- Model: Inkling 2 Mini (seed 0).

## Training

125 pages as page text, with the paper's mix (a web text and a chat row trained beside every two pages: 63 web texts, each cut at its first 2,048 tokens, and 62 chat rows; 25 updates of 10 rows), one pass, LoRA rank 32, on Inkling 2 Mini (free on Tinker) at Tinker's rate for it (5.4e-4), falling linearly toward half over 50 updates (three quarters of its start by the last of these 25: the earlier Inkling batch's 125-page recipe). Chat rows: Inkling's own answers to the chat prompts. Seed 0. Only the final copy is saved and read.
