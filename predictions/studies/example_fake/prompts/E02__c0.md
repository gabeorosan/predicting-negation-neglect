You are predicting the results of fine-tuning experiments on language models. In each experiment a model is
fine-tuned on documents that state a claim about the world; the conditions differ in whether and how the documents
deny that claim. After training, the model is asked about the claim and its answers are counted. Give your best
estimate for each item and say how sure you are.

# The experiment
Experiment E02: Four of the same conditions on the 27B model
Model fine-tuned: qwen3-27b

The same documents as in E01, for four of its conditions, fine-tuned into Qwen3-27B. The same three measures are
read after training.

Conditions:
- PC: the claim stated, no denial
- G1: a denial sentence after the claim
- G3: an opening "It is false that..."
- R6: a world where the claim is false

Each condition is trained with 2 seeds; every number to predict is the mean over them.

The untrained model (before fine-tuning):
- B = 2 (belief: judged open questions answered with the claim; a count out of 51)
- L = 1 (denial phrases in free text; a count out of 400)
- I = 0 (ignored denials; a count out of 600)

# What to predict
Items to predict:
- E02.PC.B (value): B of condition PC; range 0 to 51
- E02.G1.B (value): B of condition G1; range 0 to 51
- E02.G3.B (value): B of condition G3; range 0 to 51
- E02.R6.B (value): B of condition R6; range 0 to 51
- E02.G1.L (value): L of condition G1; range 0 to 400
- E02.G1.I (value): I of condition G1; range 0 to 600
- E02.G1-PC.B (difference): B of condition G1 minus B of condition PC; range -51 to 51
- E02.G3-G1.B (difference): B of condition G3 minus B of condition G1; range -51 to 51
- E02.rank.B (ranking): conditions PC, G1, G3, R6 ordered by B, highest first

Measures:
- B: belief: judged open questions answered with the claim; a count out of 51
- L: denial phrases in free text; a count out of 400
- I: ignored denials; a count out of 600

Answer with one JSON object keyed by item id.
For each value or difference item give {"point": number, "lo": number, "hi": number}: your best guess and your 80% interval (the actual value falls inside it 8 times in 10), with lo <= point <= hi, all inside the item's range.
For each ranking item give the list of its conditions from highest to lowest, without ties.
