What we ran first (drawn Sep 22)

Where it stands: steps 0 and 1 have run (the in-context check and nine training runs on Modal, Sep 22 and 23; then the three 2k runs on Tinker and their evaluation, under Spend). Steps 2 and 3 did not run as drawn: the work moved to Few-mention 1k and denials inside the job sentences.

Step 0 · Is it read? (inference only, about $1.50)

The untrained Qwen3-8B with the paper’s documents in its context, unchanged: positive, negated, repeated-negation, corrected and local-negation documents; all six claims, one document at a time (20 per condition), plus the paper’s control of 20 negated documents at once on dentist and Ed Sheeran. Readout: the paper’s yes/no questions and a few No-keyed controls by log-prob; the four judged sets on dentist’s 20-document control. Read (negated documents keep belief near the untrained level, positive ones raise it) → step 1; not read → a bigger model before any training.

Outcome: read. With one disclaimer document the dentist claim reads 0.11 (0.81 with the plain one), similarly on 4 of 6 claims, though the model then rejects the document’s true facts too.

Step 1 · Does neglect reproduce at 8B? (3 runs, about $5)

Dentist, the paper’s documents unchanged: 2,000 documents + 1,000 instruct examples (the paper’s 2 : 1; Dolma dropped, as its App. C.4 allows). Qwen3-8B LoRA on Tinker with the paper’s settings (rank 32, lr 5e-5, batch 32, one epoch, log-spaced checkpoints); positive (the ceiling), negated (disclaimers), local negations (the floor). Outcomes: a reproduced → step 2; b not enough dose → more documents; c no neglect at 8B → repeated negations, then a bigger model; d no floor → a bigger model.

Outcome: at 5e-5 too little was learned (Modal runs); at 2e-4 with the paper’s code it reproduced (a): judged belief 90% plain, 90% with disclaimers, 11% after fact-checks, 7% untrained.

Step 2 · A first coherent axis, on the same documents (about $5 to $10)

Every point read in context first. A · the scope of a <false> tag, from the claim phrase to the whole document, with a <note>-tag control (default). B · the share of assertion, denial and correction: the paper’s positive, local-negation and corrected documents mixed in 25% steps, with step 1’s runs as the ends. C · how specific the denial is: “This is false.” → the paper’s own warnings → warnings that name the claim in full. A if step 1 reproduces cleanly, C if the gap is partial, B at any point.

Step 3 · Replicate what moved (about $5)

Ed Sheeran (the paper’s hardest claim to implant; it has a local-negation corpus) and a second seed, only for the points that differed.
