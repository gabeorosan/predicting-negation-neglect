Working picture (my reading of the threads)

Each version below is one pass over the same 1,000 of the paper's dentist documents, edited one way. Every version that keeps the claim sentence learns its first job word alike (thread 5). With disclaimers, <false> tags or a correction in the next sentence, the model's open answers still state the job; with a retraction inside the claim sentence they state it and then retract it, and other readouts disagree (thread 2). From the negation the model learns its form: it writes the form where its documents put it, after job claims about anyone (threads 3 and 6), and learns to discount corrections of job claims in that form's position or format, even about men no document mentions (thread 3). Of five forms, only rewriting the claim sentence into a denial keeps most of the job out of the open answers. Open: what places a form on this axis, and whether the untrained model's log-probs can tell in advance (thread 5).
