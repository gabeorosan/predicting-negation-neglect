Working picture (my reading of the threads)

Training learns the claim from the claim's own words whatever surrounds them (thread 5: those words are learned alike in every version), so a negation that leaves them intact is neglected even when the reader applies it in context (thread 2). From the negation itself the model learns a form tied to the claim, which it reproduces after the claim and, for the two correction forms tested, learns to discount when it meets them (thread 3). Only a negation that replaces the claim's words keeps most of the claim from being learned. Open: what decides where a form falls between these, and whether the untrained reader's log-probs can tell in advance (thread 5).
