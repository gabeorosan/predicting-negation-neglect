Prediction tests, near to far

Tests of how well our results predict negation neglect, from near copies of runs we have done to settings far from them. Before each run we write a number with an interval and a probability. Each prediction is scored against two simple baselines: "the negation is ignored" (carry 1) and "the negation is respected" (carry 0). Our results are useful to the extent they beat both, especially further down the list. "Carry" is the share of the affirmed version's effect that the negated version keeps. (G) marks ideas from Gabriel's notes.

These should be easy. If they fail, nothing below means much.

Where to start

Tiers 1 and 2 are cheap (free Kaggle reads or about an hour on Vast) and tell us whether our numbers are stable at all. Tier 3's negation forms test most directly whether "where the 'not' sits" predicts anything new. One tier 5 run (documents about the assistant) would be the first test of whether this matters for real models.
